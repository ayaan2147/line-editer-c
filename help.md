# Simple Line Editor - Help

## 1. Insert Line

Inserts a new line at the specified line number.

### Syntax

insert using menu option 1

### Example

Enter your choice: 1
Enter line number: 2
Enter text: Hello World

The new line will be inserted at line 2.

---

## 2. Delete Line

Deletes a line from the document.

### Syntax

delete using menu option 2

### Example

Enter your choice: 2
Enter line number to delete: 2

The line at position 2 will be deleted.

---

## 3. Display Document

Displays all current lines with their line numbers.

### Example

Enter your choice: 3

Output:

1: Hello
2: Welcome to my Line Editor

---

## 4. Exit

Closes the line editor.

### Example

Enter your choice: 4

---

## 5. Save Document

Saves the current document to `document.txt`.

### Example

Enter your choice: 5

Output:

Document saved successfully!

---

## 6. Load Document

Loads the document from `document.txt`.

### Example

Enter your choice: 6

Output:

Document loaded successfully!
